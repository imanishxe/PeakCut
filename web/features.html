What you get

Built around the way clipping actually works.

Two independent judges, one decision

A text judge reads the transcript. A reaction judge watches loudness and chat activity during live streams. PeakCut cuts a clip when both see a moment, which is why the results read as highlights rather than noise.

Words judgeReaction judgeAgreement = clip

Works before the stream ends

Live watch records the last stretch of a stream, checks it as often as every minute and produces clips while the creator is still talking.

Live

Replays and uploads too

YouTube, Twitch and Kick links or your own video file. Add several sources and each keeps its own set of clips.

Word-by-word captions

Captions are timed to the voice, with key words highlighted and a hook line above the clip. Three styles: Bounce, Hype and Clean.

Copy that ships with the clip

Every clip gets a hook, title, description and hashtags, written from what was actually said. Reword any of them before posting.

Edit without starting over

Trim the start and end, rewrite the copy, change the caption style or layout, then re-make the clip from the same source.

Post straight to Shorts and Reels

Queue approved clips for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels from the same workspace. Download keeps a local copy for everywhere else.

Many creators, one server

Give each creator their own workspace with separate style and posting settings. Useful for agencies and channels with several shows.

AI usage you can read

Each call is logged with creator, video and tokens. See what your key spent before you look at your provider's bill.

Your key, your rules

The AI is yours to choose. So is the bill.

Add a key for Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Claude, OpenRouter or Groq; point at Ollama on your own machine; or use any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Without a key, PeakCut still clips by loudness.

Custom endpoints

Set your own base address, model list and key. Local models and self-hosted proxies work.

Transcription stays local

Audio is processed on the machine running PeakCut, not uploaded to a caption service.

No PeakCut invoice

There is no PeakCut subscription. You pay your AI provider directly for the calls you make.
