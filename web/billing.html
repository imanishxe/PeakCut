How checkout works

No silent charges. No fake success.

Paid access is granted only after the payment provider confirms a subscription to the server.

01 · The operator configures payment

An operator sets Stripe credentials and paid plan prices in the server environment. No key is ever sent to the browser.

PEAKCUT_BILLING_PROVIDER=stripe
PEAKCUT_BILLING_SECRET_KEY=…
PEAKCUT_BILLING_PRICE_PRO=…
02 · Your browser asks the server

This page loads /api/billing/plans. Checkout buttons stay disabled unless the server reports that checkout is available.

03 · Stripe hosts the payment

The server creates a Checkout session and redirects you to Stripe's secure site. PeakCut never sees your card details.

04 · A signed webhook unlocks access

Only a verified subscription event from Stripe changes your account's entitlements. Cancelling is handled through your Stripe subscription.

Questions

Billing, without the fine print.

Is PeakCut really free?

Yes. The software is free and open source and runs on your own computer or server. There is no mandatory PeakCut subscription.

What does “bring your own key” mean?

You supply an API key for the AI service you choose in Settings → AI. PeakCut uses it to select moments and write hooks, titles and captions. You pay that provider directly for the calls you make.

What happens if I have no AI key?

Clips are still made. PeakCut picks loud moments that contain speech, and you can add a key later for AI selection and copy.

Why is checkout disabled here?

Because this server has no configured payment credentials or paid plan prices. The page says so instead of pretending a subscription was created. Free and BYOK remain available.

Who pays for storage and bandwidth?

The person running the server. Self-hosting keeps your videos and clips on your own machine; a hosted operator may offer paid plans with different limits.
