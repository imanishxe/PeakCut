Recent changes

Newest first. This page is updated when changes ship, not before.

23 November 2026

Public pages, rebuilt for reading

A new landing page, Features, Billing & Plans, Guides and this changelog share one design: PeakCut orange and Geist, a translucent navbar that appears on scroll, a working mobile menu and the PixelDreamStudios footer.

  • NewBilling explains Free and bring-your-own-key honestly; checkout stays off until the server has payment credentials.
  • NewGuides follow the real app controls from upload to posting.
  • ImprovedScroll reveals respect reduced-motion settings, and the menu closes on link click or Escape.
21 November 2026

Chat spikes and live watcher mode

Live clipping now treats chat activity as a reaction signal alongside loudness, so a sudden surge in chat can trigger a fresh check even between scheduled intervals.

  • NewReaction judge watches chat activity during live streams.
20 November 2026

More languages, better post text

Arabic speech support joined the per-language model list, and generated post text keeps the game rules while hashtags move into the description.

  • NewArabic speech model pinned to one checked file.
  • ImprovedThree to five fitting hashtags added to a clip's description.
  • ImprovedGenerated post text respects the configured game name.
20 November 2026

Uploads and AI calls got steadier

Leftover uploads no longer block a new one, and AI rate limits are waited out properly instead of failing a job.

  • FixedStale upload state no longer blocks the next video.
  • FixedAI rate limits are retried with a wait.
  • FixedModel discovery timeout and validation, live ingest stop cleanup and long-video handling.
19 November 2026

Your words, your clip names

Reusable text and sensible filenames arrived: one description for every clip, your own big text, and downloads named after the clip title.

  • NewWrite one description used on every clip.
  • NewSet your own big text on every clip.
  • ImprovedDownloads use the clip title instead of “clip01.mp4”.
Want the details? Every note above comes from a commit in the PeakCut repository. Features that are still being built are not listed here.
