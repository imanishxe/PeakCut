Features

The parts of clipping that eat your week.

All features

Scored by two separate judges

One AI reads only what was said. Another sees how loud the streamer got. During a live stream a clip is made when both agree, so you get moments, not filler.

Captions, styled once

Word-by-word captions are burned into every clip with key words highlighted and a hook line on top. Pick a style per creator and every clip follows it.

Live clipping

Clips while they are still live

Add a streamer's channel and PeakCut can record when they go live. It keeps the last stretch of stream, checks it as often as every minute, and has clips ready before the stream ends.

Every AI call accounted for

Each AI call is listed with its creator, video and tokens, so you always know what your key is doing. Without a key, clips fall back to loudness picking.

FAQ

Got any questions left?

The things people ask before their first stream goes through PeakCut. More answers in the guides .

Is PeakCut free?

Yes. PeakCut is free and open source. You run it on your own computer or server. There is no PeakCut subscription; see Billing & Plans.

Which AI does it use?

Your own. Add a key for Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Claude, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint. You pay that provider directly.

What happens without an AI key?

PeakCut still makes clips, picking the loudest moments that have speech in them. Add a key and the AI chooses the moments and writes hooks and titles.

Which videos work best?

Anything with people talking and reacting: live streams, gaming VODs, podcasts, interviews. Links can be from YouTube, Twitch or Kick, or you can upload a video file. Captions are written from English speech.

How long are the clips, and what shape?

Vertical 9:16, the shape Shorts, Reels and TikTok use. They run 15 to 60 seconds by default and you can set your own range. Choose a blurred fill, a zoomed frame or a centre crop.

Can I change a clip afterwards?

Yes. Open any clip to trim the start and end, rewrite the hook and title, or switch caption style and layout, then re-make it.

Can I clip any streamer?

PeakCut is a tool. You are responsible for having the right to clip and post a creator's content; most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
